# Hi, I'm Thibault

I'm a final-year engineering student at UTC (Université de Technologie de Compiègne), on the Data & AI track. This semester I'm on exchange at Université Laval in Québec, taking NoSQL databases, cloud and DevOps.

What I like about data is making it talk. A pile of raw tables doesn't answer anything until someone cleans it, models it and asks it a real question. I try to start from the need (who will use this, and for what?) and work back to the pipeline, rather than the other way round. That's also why I care about data quality: if people can't trust the numbers, the rest doesn't matter.

## Featured projects

[chess-elo-drift](https://github.com/Menak49/chess-elo-drift): does a chess rating mean the same thing in 2014 as in 2026? I re-analysed thousands of chess.com and Lichess games with one fixed Stockfish and compared how accurately a given rating plays, year by year. My first version of the study was wrong (chess.com reclassified 10-minute games in 2020). I kept it in the repo, along with the explanation, because I learned more from that mistake than from the result.

[rag-recettes](https://github.com/Menak49/rag-recettes) (RAGout): a recipe chatbot, built in a team of 4 to answer a simple need, "what do I cook with what's in my fridge?". We scraped and structured recipes from 750g.com, embedded them in ChromaDB and added a corrective RAG (CRAG) step on top of Gemini. [Try the demo](https://duflan.streamlit.app/).
